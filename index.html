Pathway-Mapping Tool

A practical framework for mapping industry competencies to education, assigning partner roles, reducing duplication, and opening multiple entry points into technician careers.

Download the tool (Excel)

Works in Excel or Google Sheets. Best used on a laptop.

Five steps, one worksheet each

  1. Define the roles. Start from the jobs employers hire for, not from programs.
  2. Map competencies to education. Build one competency spine and place every offering on it.
  3. Assign partner roles. One clear lead for every function.
  4. Find overlaps and gaps. Coordinate the overlaps; fill the holes.
  5. Open multiple entry points. Many doors onto one pathway, with job value at every stop.

Adapting it to your sector

Rename the role families, replace the competency rows with your sector’s, and overwrite the Arizona example entries. The overlap checks and summary update automatically.

The starter competency spine is drawn from the SciTech Institute Semiconductor Manufacturing Skills Crosswalk.
